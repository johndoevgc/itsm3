1. Welcome 👋

This portal is your one-stop window for anything IT-related — broken laptop, password reset, new software, access request, or just a question. You don't need to know any technical jargon. The portal (and our AI assistant) will guide you, ask the right questions, and route your ticket to the correct engineer.

In plain language: Think of it like ordering food online — you describe what you need, click a button, and you can track the status until it's "delivered" (resolved).

2. Signing in

  1. Open your browser and go to https://vgc-itsm1-app.azurewebsites.net
  2. Click “Sign in with Microsoft” (single sign-on with your work account).
  3. If this is your first visit you may be asked to confirm your name, email and department — these prefill on every ticket so you don't have to retype them.
Tip: Bookmark the page. On a phone you can also “Add to Home Screen” so it behaves like an app (works offline for browsing your past tickets).

7. Method C — 💬 Chat with Support (AI Assistant)

The AI assistant is the fastest way to get an answer. It's available 24×7, in plain English, and can do three things for you:

  1. Answer your question instantly from our knowledge base (e.g. "How do I connect to VPN from home?").
  2. Diagnose your issue by asking follow-up questions and giving step-by-step fixes.
  3. Open a ticket for you if it can't fix it — pre-filled from the conversation, no form to type.

8. Tracking your tickets

Open the 🎫 My Tickets tab. Each ticket shows:

  • Status dot 🟢 New · 🟡 Assigned · 🟠 In Progress · 🔵 Pending You · ✅ Resolved · ⚫ Closed
  • SLA timer — when the engineer is committed to respond / fix it.
  • Assigned engineer + their photo.
  • Updates timeline — every reply, status change, and AI suggestion is logged.

Replying to your engineer

Click any ticket → scroll to the comment box at the bottom → type your reply or attach a file. The engineer is notified immediately.

Stuck waiting? If the status shows “Pending You”, the engineer needs information from you. Reply in the comment box — the SLA clock pauses while it's on you.

9. Self-help — Knowledge Base 📚

Before opening a ticket, search the Knowledge Base tab. Most "how do I…" questions have a 2-minute answer here.

  • Type a question in the search box at the top — try natural language: "how to install Microsoft Teams".
  • Articles are tagged with read-time (e.g. 3 min) and freshness (Updated today).
  • The 🤖 Ask AI box at the top of the KB summarises multiple articles into a single answer with citations.
Why use it: Self-served answers are instant — no waiting for an engineer.

10. Tips for faster resolution ⚡

  1. Be specific. "Outlook crashes every time I open a meeting" is 10× better than "email broken".
  2. Include exact error messages (copy-paste). Codes like 0x80004005 point straight at a known fix.
  3. Attach a screenshot. A 2-second screenshot saves a 5-minute back-and-forth.
  4. Pick the right urgency. Don't mark everything P1 — see the table below.
  5. Reply quickly when status is “Pending You”. The clock is paused, but your day isn't.
  6. Use chat first for "how do I…" questions — instant answer, no ticket needed.

11. How priority is decided

You pick an Urgency; the AI combines it with Impact (how many people are affected) to set the final Priority.

P1 — Critical

Whole team / site down. Response in 15 min, fix in 4 h.

P2 — High

You can't work, others can. Response 30 min, fix 8 h.

P3 — Medium

Annoying but you can work around it. Response 2 h, fix 2 days.

P4 — Low

Cosmetic / future request. Response next business day.

SLA times shown are typical — your contract may differ.

13. Frequently Asked Questions

Q. Is the AI a real person?

No — it's an AI assistant trained on our internal IT knowledge. But every ticket it creates is reviewed by a real engineer.

Q. Will my chat be read by humans?

Engineers can see the chat only if a ticket is opened, so they have context. Otherwise it stays between you and the AI. Sensitive data (IDs, card numbers) is automatically redacted.

Q. Can I use the portal on my phone?

Yes — it's mobile-friendly. You can also "Add to Home Screen" for an app-like icon, and browse your past tickets even when offline.

Q. What if the AI gets it wrong?

Click “✗ Still broken — open ticket” in the chat, or use the 👎 button on any answer. A human engineer takes over and the AI learns from the correction.

Q. I urgently need help — should I still chat first?

For genuine P1 emergencies (site down, security incident), use 🎫 Report an Issue and set Urgency = "Critical". For everything else, chat is fastest.

Q. Can I CC my manager?

Yes — open the ticket, click “Add watcher” and search their name. They get every update by email.

Q. How do I cancel a request?

Open it in My Requests → click Cancel. If it's already approved/in fulfilment, add a comment and the engineer will close it.

Q. Where can I see service outages?

The portal home shows a banner if any major service (email, VPN, network) is degraded — no need to log a ticket if it's a known outage.
